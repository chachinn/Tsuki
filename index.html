GENERAL REFERENCE

WHO antenatal contacts

🌍

WHO's global antenatal-care model recommends a minimum of eight contacts, beginning in the first trimester. Your own maternity provider may recommend a different schedule based on your health, country, and pregnancy.

⚠️ Urgent maternal warning signs

Seek medical care immediately for urgent warning signs such as persistent/worsening headache, vision changes, fainting, fever ≥38°C, trouble breathing, chest pain, severe persistent abdominal pain, significant bleeding/fluid leakage, extreme swelling, severe nausea/vomiting, or a meaningful reduction in your baby's usual movement.

Based on CDC Hear Her urgent maternal warning signs. This list is not exhaustive.

Dating note

First-trimester ultrasound is the most accurate method to establish or confirm gestational age. ART pregnancies should use ART-derived dating. Once your clinician establishes an EDD, Tsuki should not keep changing it from later calendar guesses.
